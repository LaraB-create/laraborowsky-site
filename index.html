Chapter II · The card catalog

Six things I built,filed properly.

Each one gets a real Dewey call number, because I’m the kind of person who would. The demos are being rebuilt from scratch on Plan B Partners, a fictional firm, so nothing you see comes from a real client.

658.8 POSMarketing & Brand

Positioning Discovery

Built because “trusted partner” is not a position.

A structured way to figure out what a firm should actually be known for, where every recommendation has to trace back to evidence.

How it works

Claims go in a ledger with their source. Candidate positions get pressure-tested against the proof before anyone falls in love with one.

Margin note

The demo runs it on Plan B Partners’ own identity crisis: are they the backup-plan people, or a mobility strategy firm? (The rabbit has opinions.)

  • Research design
  • Claude
741.6 BRAMarketing & Brand

Brand Design System

Built because I’m one person, and there are a lot of slides.

A brand kit written so an AI design tool can follow it, which means everyone’s output comes out on brand.

How it works

Colors, type, layouts, voice and accessibility rules, written down once in a form the AI can follow.

Margin note

Accessibility lives in the templates, not in a checklist at the end. If the default is accessible, nobody has to remember to be.

  • Brand systems
  • Accessibility
658.403 GNGBD & Pursuit

Go/No-Go Portal

Built because “gut feel” is not a bid strategy.

A screening tool that recommends whether to chase a solicitation, and shows every reason behind the recommendation.

How it works

It checks fit, scores the criteria, and won’t cite anything it can’t trace to a record. A person makes the final call.

Margin note

The first version gave a single score. Nobody trusted it. A number without its reasons is just a vibe.

  • Decision support
  • Claude
658.81 LEABD & Pursuit

Lead Management

Built because nobody should read procurement alerts before coffee.

Turns a firehose of procurement alerts into a short list people actually read.

How it works

A regular pre-screen, a roundup, and a log of every cut, so you can see what got filtered out and why, and argue with it.

Margin note

The demo includes duplicate and stale leads on purpose. A triage tool with nothing to triage proves nothing.

  • Automation
  • Python
005.74 DATData & Systems

Data Portal

Built because three spreadsheets disagreed, and all three were confident.

Scattered records rebuilt into one source a proposal team can trust.

How it works

Sources get reconciled, every field gets a clear source of truth, and whatever still doesn’t match lands on a worklist.

Margin note

Someday you’ll click a drawer and the complete record opens. That’s the goal, and I’m stubborn about it.

  • Data
  • Python
006.3 ACAFrom the lab

Claude Mastery Academy

Built because motivation is a design problem.

A gamified learning portal I built for myself, complete with XP. It’s where I learn the tools that build the other tools.

How it works

Phases, quizzes, flashcards, and a deep-dive button on every concept, so curiosity is the curriculum instead of a distraction from it.

Margin note

I learn by falling into things. So I built something worth falling into.

  • React
  • Personal project
